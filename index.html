AutoRide - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

AutoRide - Driver Assistance ("AutoRide", "we", "our", or "the app") is an application designed to assist drivers while using supported ride-hailing applications.

1. Information We Collect

AutoRide may collect and process a device identifier used to identify the installation/device for application activation, licensing, and subscription management.

The application uses the Android device identifier ANDROID_ID as part of its licensing system.

AutoRide may also process information that is displayed on the screen of supported applications through Android's Accessibility Service. This information is processed by the application to perform its driver-assistance functionality.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected by AutoRide may be used to:

  • Activate and validate application licenses.
  • Maintain subscription and trial status.
  • Associate a license with a device.
  • Provide the application's driver-assistance functionality.
  • Maintain application functionality and security.

3. Accessibility Service

AutoRide uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect relevant information displayed by supported applications and to perform actions requested by the application's functionality.

AutoRide does not use the Accessibility Service to collect information unrelated to the application's intended functionality.

4. Firebase and Third-Party Services

AutoRide uses Firebase services, including Firebase Firestore, to store and process information required for application activation, licensing, trials, and subscriptions.

Data processed through Firebase is handled according to Google's Firebase privacy and security practices.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by AutoRide. Data transmitted between the application and supported cloud services is transmitted using secure network connections.

6. Data Retention

License, activation, and related information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide licensing, subscription, security, and support functionality.

7. Data Deletion

Users may contact us to request deletion of information associated with their AutoRide installation or license, subject to any information that we may be required to retain for legitimate legal or security purposes.

For deletion or privacy-related requests, please contact us using the support contact provided on the application's Google Play listing.

8. Children's Privacy

AutoRide is not directed toward children and is intended for use by drivers and other adult users.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests regarding AutoRide, please contact the developer using the support email provided on the Google Play Store listing.
